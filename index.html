The concept

Not just a cup.

Chip n´ Sip turns the container into the best part of the experience. Why throw away the cup when you can eat it?

1

Fill it.

Scoop, pour or drizzle. The cup holds it all.

2

Sip it.

Enjoy your drink or dessert, one sip at a time.

3

Crunch it.

And then, the best part: eat the cup.

A sweeter side effect

What if the cup was part of the dessert?

Disposable cups are used for minutes and then they're gone. Chip n´ Sip aims to reduce our reliance on single-use containers by making the cup something you actually enjoy eating.
